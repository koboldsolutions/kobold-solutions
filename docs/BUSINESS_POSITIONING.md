# Kobold Solutions business positioning

## Audience and voice

Kobold is a technical partner for business owners. Lead with practical needs and outcomes, then explain the technical work. Describe both consulting and hands-on implementation. The AI offering focuses on development teams and includes related internal workflow automation.

Do not promise specific productivity gains, guaranteed results, certifications, or customer outcomes that have not been established. Explain MCP as connections that let AI tools work with a company's tools and information. Avoid presenting e-commerce or CRMs as standalone offerings.

## Canonical service order and content sources

1. `development`: Custom Software Development / Desarrollo de Software a Medida
2. `consulting`: Software Consulting / Consultoría de Software
3. `ai`: AI for Engineering Teams / IA para Equipos de Desarrollo

`src/common/services.js` defines identity, order, and image references. Both `public/locales/{en,es}/common.json` dictionaries contain the copy. Landing/service cards share `ServiceCards`; detailed sections and orbit controls use the same registry. Keep all surfaces in this order.

The logo cube is accompanied by a browser window for custom software, a diagnostic panel for consulting, and connected nodes for AI. Preserve its existing interaction and reduced-motion behavior.

## Boundaries

- Historical portfolio entries and e-commerce articles remain accurate and retain their routes.
- Contact details and the free 15-minute introductory call are unchanged.
- Existing service imagery is retained; the shopping-bag model is replaced.
- The AI offer is engineering-first, with related internal automation; it is not a promise to solve every possible AI use case.
- Existing design, bilingual persistence, static export, and GitHub Pages deployment are preserved.

We build custom software, solve technical challenges, and help your team put AI to work. From choosing the right approach to implementing it, we handle the technical details with you.

**Introduction:** Move your business forward with the right technology

Whether you need new software, a clearer technical direction, or help adopting AI, we work with you to understand the problem and deliver a practical solution. We explain the options, handle the technical work, and help your team take ownership.

#### Custom Software Development

Software built around how your business works. We develop applications, internal tools, and integrations that solve your specific challenges—from the first idea to implementation.

**Detail heading:** Software built around your business.

Start with what your business needs, not a predefined platform. We turn requirements into applications and connected systems, improve existing software, and prepare your team to run what we build.

- Requirements discovery and project definition
- Custom web and business applications
- Internal tools for everyday operations
- Integrations between your tools and systems
- Modernization of existing software
- Deployment, documentation, and handover

#### Software Consulting

Clear guidance for your next technical decision. We assess existing systems, identify improvements, and help you choose and implement the right approach.

**Detail heading:** A clear path through technical decisions.

Understand what is working, what needs attention, and where to invest next. We review your systems and priorities, explain the tradeoffs, and support implementation with your team.

- Assessment of existing software and systems
- Architecture review and design guidance
- Technology and platform selection
- Performance analysis and optimization
- Prioritized technical roadmaps
- Hands-on implementation support

#### AI for Engineering Teams

Help your development team put AI to work. We assess your workflow, set up AI coding tools, and connect them to your systems through MCP servers. We provide technical guidance and hands-on implementation, including related internal automation.

**Detail heading:** Put AI to work with your team.

We help your development team adopt AI in the work it already does. That includes AI-assisted coding, practical integrations, and related internal automation. MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers connect AI tools to your company’s tools and information. We assess what fits, implement the connections, and help your team use them.

- Assessment of development workflows and AI opportunities
- Setup of AI-assisted coding tools
- Custom MCP server development and configuration
- Integrations with team tools and project context
- Automation of related internal workflows
- Team onboarding, documentation, and handover

**Process**

- **Discovery:** We start by understanding your business, existing systems, and the challenge you want to solve.
- **Recommendations:** We explain the options and tradeoffs, then agree on a practical approach and clear priorities.
- **Implementation:** We build, configure, and integrate the solution, working with your team and checking it against your needs.
- **Handover:** We document the work and help your team use and maintain the solution. Together, we define the next steps.

**Philosophy**

- We work with you to understand your business and technical constraints. Your team stays involved as we define priorities and implement the solution.
- We choose tools for the problem they solve. That includes AI when it is useful, with a clear understanding of its limitations and the work involved.
- We explain recommendations, tradeoffs, and progress in plain language so you can make informed decisions.
- We document what we build and share the knowledge your team needs to use it, maintain it, and decide what comes next.

**Contact:** Your next technical challenge, solved together.

Need custom software, guidance on an existing system, or help bringing AI into your team? Tell us what you want to achieve. We’ll work with you to define the next step.
