01. Custom Software Development

Software built around your business.

Start with what your business needs, not a predefined platform. We turn requirements into applications and connected systems, improve existing software, and prepare your team to run what we build.

  • Requirements discovery and project definition
  • Custom web and business applications
  • Internal tools for everyday operations
  • Integrations between your tools and systems
  • Modernization of existing software
  • Deployment, documentation, and handover
02. Software Consulting

A clear path through technical decisions.

Understand what is working, what needs attention, and where to invest next. We review your systems and priorities, explain the tradeoffs, and support implementation with your team.

  • Assessment of existing software and systems
  • Architecture review and design guidance
  • Technology and platform selection
  • Performance analysis and optimization
  • Prioritized technical roadmaps
  • Hands-on implementation support
03. AI for Engineering Teams

Put AI to work with your team.

We help your development team adopt AI in the work it already does. That includes AI-assisted coding, practical integrations, and related internal automation. MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers connect AI tools to your company’s tools and information. We assess what fits, implement the connections, and help your team use them.

  • Assessment of development workflows and AI opportunities
  • Setup of AI-assisted coding tools
  • Custom MCP server development and configuration
  • Integrations with team tools and project context
  • Automation of related internal workflows
  • Team onboarding, documentation, and handover

We work with you to understand your business and technical constraints. Your team stays involved as we define priorities and implement the solution.
